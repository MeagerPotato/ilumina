# Backup demo video (1 minute, optional)

Optional on Devpost, but it's the backup if the live demo fails in front of the judges. Keep it to 1 minute. Say the points in your own words.

## Before you press record

1. In Terminal, in the ilumina folder: `ILUMINA_CAMERA=gopro uv run bot.py`
2. Wait about 20 seconds, then open http://localhost:7860/dashboard/ in Chrome, full screen.
3. GoPro on the headband, plugged in by USB. Write the problem big with a thick marker, within about 30 cm of the camera.
4. Focus mode on so no notifications pop up.
5. Sound: QuickTime records only the mic, not the Mac's sound, so play Ilumina through the MacBook speakers. Do a 20 second test; if Ilumina hears itself, film the screen with your iPhone instead.
6. QuickTime, File, New Screen Recording, Options, Microphone: MacBook microphone, Record.

## Shots and talking points

**1. Hook and start (0:00–0:10)** · *Do: click Start session, Ilumina greets you*
- AI tutors hear what you say, Ilumina hears how you say it
- Voice tutor on Pipecat, Deepgram and Gemini 4

**2. Camera (0:10–0:22)** · *Do: Turn camera on, show the handwritten problem, ask for help*
- GoPro on your head shows it your page
- Gemini 4 reads the handwriting and points at the exact step

**3. Hesitation (0:22–0:45)** · *Do: answer slowly with a long pause, twice. Point at the dashboard*
- Flux word timings catch the long pause
- Gemini scores confusion from your voice, with how sure it is
- Two hesitant turns = trend, so Ilumina explains it differently and writes the math on the board

**4. Lecture memory (0:45–0:55)** · **(PR 17–19)** *Do: Lecture review switch or the memory panel.* If not merged, give these seconds to shot 3
- Vexo open kit: it listened to the lecture and reviews it with you
- Nothing saved without a yes

**5. Close (0:55–1:00)** · *Show: end card "Ilumina · Pipecat · Deepgram · Gemini 4 · Vexo"*
- A tutor that notices when you're lost

## After recording

- Trim in QuickTime (Edit, Trim). Upload to YouTube as Unlisted and paste the link into Devpost.
- Keep the file on the desktop to play offline if the live demo breaks.
